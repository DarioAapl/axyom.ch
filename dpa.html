1. Roles

For personal data relating to your customers and store visitors, you are the controller and Axyom is the processor. If we believe an instruction infringes data protection law, we will tell you.

DurationFor as long as the app is installed or your account exists, plus the erasure periods in section 8.

6. International transfers

Transfers to the United States rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which are incorporated in our data processing agreement with OpenAI. Switzerland benefits from an adequacy decision; Canada benefits from an adequacy decision for commercial organisations.

7. Security

Taking account of the state of the art, cost, and the risks to data subjects, we implement:

  • Encryption in transit — TLS on every connection.
  • Encryption at rest — the data volume is encrypted, and authorisation tokens are additionally encrypted at the application layer. API keys and session identifiers are stored only as cryptographic hashes.
  • Access control — production access limited to the processor; API keys scoped to a specific merchant domain and refused from any other origin.
  • Data minimisation by design — the order query is written to exclude name, email, telephone and address.
  • Resilience and restoration — backups held on the same encrypted volume, restorable on request.
  • Testing — an automated test suite covering the security boundaries above runs before each deployment.

9. Assisting you

We will help you meet your obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us. If a data subject contacts us directly, we will not respond substantively but will refer them to you and tell you promptly.

10. Personal data breaches

We will notify you without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, with the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken.

11. Audits

We will make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum, and will accept audits by you or an auditor you mandate, on reasonable notice, no more than once per year unless a breach or a supervisory authority requires otherwise, and subject to confidentiality.

12. Precedence

Where this Addendum conflicts with our Terms of Service, this Addendum prevails for matters of personal data processing.
